## 5. Reviews

- Rating **4.9** verified on 27 Oct 2026 (static value, labelled with its date on the site).
- Review count and review texts **could not be verified** without an authenticated or API source. **No reviews or names are shown.**
- The site includes a server-side function (`api/google-reviews.ts`) that fetches the live rating, count and up to 5 reviews from the Google Places API (New), with the key kept on the server and 12-hour CDN caching. Once `GOOGLE_PLACES_API_KEY` and `GOOGLE_PLACE_ID` are configured on the host, real reviews appear with Google author attribution.
